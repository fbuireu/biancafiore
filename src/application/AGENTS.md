# src/application

The anti-corruption layer between Contentful and the domain. `dto/` maps and `entities/` loads. See ADR 0012 for the layering, and ADR 0002 for why the content is in Contentful at all: this folder is what keeps that choice reversible.

## dto/ (Contentful → domain)

```
dto/<concept>/
  <concept>DTO.ts   # export function createXs(raw: RawX[]): XDTO[]
  types.ts          # XSkeleton (EntrySkeletonType) + RawX = Entry<XSkeleton, undefined>
  utils/            # mapping helpers owned by this concept
  index.ts          # re-exports the DTO only
```

A DTO fed by more than one query takes one object naming each array, as `createAuthors({ rawAuthors, rawArticles })` and `createTagIndex({ rawTags, rawArticles, rawAuthors })` do; the loader fills it by destructuring the tuple `fetchEntries` answers, in query order.

The `authors` and `tags` loaders fetch their secondary content types with a `select` list, typed `SelectedField<Skeleton>` ([`dto/shared/select.ts`](./dto/shared/select.ts)) and declared beside the helper that reads those fields: `AUTHOR_LATEST_ARTICLE_FIELDS` beside `getLatestArticleByAuthor`, `TAG_INDEX_ARTICLE_FIELDS` and `TAG_INDEX_AUTHOR_FIELDS` beside `getTags` and `getAuthors`. A list names every field its helpers reach, through `orderArticleReferences` and `articleReference` too, `sys.id` among them, which is what `articleSlug` and `articlePublishDateISO` name a refused entry by. The raw type does not narrow to the selection, so a helper that starts reading a field its list omits still type-checks and reads `undefined` in production: grow the list in the same change.

- **DTOs are pure on purpose.** They take already-fetched raw entries and return domain DTOs: no `getEntries`, no Effect, no env, no `await` on I/O. Every `create` is synchronous, and deterministic: a date leaves as a UTC instant (`publishDateISO`) or a UTC year (`createPeriod`), so the time zone the build runs in never moves it. Nor does a mapper order anything itself: the Blog's order is `sortFavoriteFirst` and the Latest Article is the head of `sortReverseChronological`, both domain rules. The line is I/O, not layering: `@infrastructure/images/imageOptimization` *is* imported here (`getOptimizedImageUrl`, `getOptimizedSrcset` and `getOriginImageUrl` only build a CDN URL string), while `getImagePlaceholders`, which fetches, is not.
- **Contentful types stop here.** `EntryFieldTypes`, `EntrySkeletonType`, `documentToHtmlString` may appear in this folder and nowhere downstream. The domain never sees a `sys` or a `fields`.
- **Where each mapping lives.** A `utils/` helper lives under the DTO it serves, and one that several DTOs share lives under the concept it maps. `createAuthor` in [`dto/author/utils/author.ts`](./dto/author/utils/author.ts) turns a raw author entry into the fields a Byline and the authors collection carry: `createAuthors` spreads it and adds the Latest Article, and `createArticles` calls it for the Byline it embeds. The Author Tag cannot call it, because `createAuthor` also maps the profile image and `getAuthors` reads Authors selected down to a name and a slug, so both take the part they share from `authorIdentity` beside it, the trimmed name and slug, and `articleAuthorSlug` reads the slug of the Author an Article links, which the Latest Article and the Author Tag both match on. `tagIdentity` and `articleTagSlugs` in [`dto/tag/utils/tag.ts`](./dto/tag/utils/tag.ts) do the same for a Tag and for the Tags an Article carries, so Related Articles and the Tag Index match on one trimmed string. Both identities refuse a name or a slug that is only padding, naming the entry, since the Tag Index files an entry under the first letter of its name and a page needs a slug, and `articleIsFavorite` beside `articleSlug` reads the Favorite flag. `articleSlug` in [`dto/article/utils/reference.ts`](./dto/article/utils/reference.ts) turns a raw Article entry into the string that identifies it, and `articleReference` beside it into a `Reference<"articles">`, so no `{ collection: "articles" }` literal is written anywhere else in this folder, and `articlePublishDateISO` beside them reads its publish date through the domain's `publishDateISO` and rethrows a refusal that names the entry by its slug and `sys.id`, the domain's own as its `cause`, so a build that stops on a bad date says which entry to open (`createCities` does the same for a City's Period, naming the City); `createArticles` takes its own `slug` from the same call, and the rich-text renderer links an embedded Article through it. `createRelatedArticles` in [`dto/article/utils/articles.ts`](./dto/article/utils/articles.ts) answers what an Article's Related Articles are, hand-picked or else inferred from shared Tags, and leaves the Article out of its own list in both branches, by `articleSlug`. The cap, `INFERRED_RELATED_ARTICLES_LIMIT` (6), applies to the inferred branch only.
- **Every Slug is trimmed here**, and so are the Author and Tag names the Tag Index sorts and the keys of the other collections: a City's name, a Project's `id` and a Quotee's name. The consumer, `resolveArticle`/`resolveArticles` in `@modules/core/utils/entries`, drops a reference it cannot find without a word, so a reference built from a slug Contentful padded would drop an Article from its own tag pages and empty the Author's Latest Article, silently.
- **Contentful answers a link rather than an entry** whenever the target is unpublished or the include depth is exhausted, and the type is a union that says so. `createAuthor` refuses an unresolved author link by its `sys.id`, and `createImage` refuses an Asset that is an unresolved link or carries no usable file by its `sys.id` too; an Article's Tags and Related Articles, and the author link the Tag Index and the Latest Article match on, narrow on `"fields" in entry` and drop the unresolved half. The rich-text renderer drops an unresolved embed and keeps the label of a link to one.
- **What Contentful types loosely is checked against a zod schema before it is read.** [`dto/shared/images.ts`](./dto/shared/images.ts) declares `assetFileSchema`, the file an Asset carries, once for both of its readers. `createImage` checks the Asset with `imageAssetSchema`, which extends it with what an image needs: a resolved Asset whose file has a string `url`, a `contentType` and numeric pixel dimensions. An Asset without dimensions (an SVG, a PDF) is refused there by its `sys.id`, since `imageSchema` and the `<Image>` that renders it need both. `@contentful/rich-text-types` types every node's `data` as `Record<string, any>`, so each renderer in [`dto/article/utils/content.ts`](./dto/article/utils/content.ts) checks it the same way first: a hyperlink's `uri`, an embedded or linked entry's `sys.contentType` and the fields that content type renders, an asset's `file` against `assetFileSchema` with a non-empty `url`. A node that fails renders what the same renderer renders for an incomplete one, nothing for an embed and the bare label for a link, so a dimension that is not a number never reaches a `width` attribute. A layout is checked against `IMAGE_EMBED_LAYOUT` rather than looked up by its raw string, so a key every object inherits (`toString`) names no wrapper class.
- **An asset URL is absolutised here**, by `absoluteAssetUrl` in [`dto/shared/images.ts`](./dto/shared/images.ts), which turns the protocol-relative `//images.ctfassets.net/…` Contentful serves into an `https:` URL and leaves a URL that is already whole as it is. `createImage` calls it, so `imageSchema.url` is an absolute URL and no page, component or JSON-LD builder adds the scheme, and so does the rich-text renderer in [`dto/article/utils/content.ts`](./dto/article/utils/content.ts) for its own embedded-asset URLs, which come off the raw Contentful node and never reach `createImage`. `@infrastructure/images/imageOptimization` takes a URL that is absolute or root-relative and adds nothing to it.
- **The rich-text renderer links through `@const/routes.ts`**: an embedded Article with `articleHref`, a tag page recognised with `isTagPath`. Its own `PRODUCTION_HOSTNAME`, the host an authored link is resolved against to decide whether it is external, is deliberately *not* the site origin `absoluteUrl` uses: it asks whether the editor typed a link to the production domain, which is the same question in every environment, preview included.
- **Every heading's anchor is unique in its Article and never empty.** The renderer derives it once, with `createAnchors` in [`dto/article/utils/content.ts`](./dto/article/utils/content.ts), from the heading's text through `slugify`: a repeat takes `-2`, `-3` and so on, the h1 counting among the headings, and a heading `slugify` strips whole (only punctuation, or a script outside Latin) takes `section`, numbered the same way, so the Table of Contents entry and the body spell one id. Such a heading reads `section` and not a transliteration, which stays a question for multi-language.
- **A generic iframe leaves the renderer wrapped in a block, a video does not.** `iframeEmbed` renders as `<div class="iframe-embed"><iframe …></iframe></div>` (`IFRAME_EMBED_CLASS` in [`dto/article/utils/content.ts`](./dto/article/utils/content.ts)) and `videoEmbed` as the bare iframe, so `_article.css` can size the generic one from its wrapper: a 16:9 box today, the height of its page once that page opts in (`frame-sizing: content-height`, see the modules guide). The docs test holds the class the renderer emits to the stylesheet.
- **The HTML the renderer emits is styled only by [`_article.css`](../pages/articles/_article.css)**: `article__heading`, `external-link-icon`, `split`, `split__content`, `split__image`, `full-bleed`, `breakout`, and the `--is` scope each section carries, which that stylesheet turns into a view timeline. No test reads the two files together, so renaming one side stops the other matching without failing anything.
- **Optional CMS fields get their defaults in the DTO**, so the domain DTO is total: `?? false` for the editorial flags an author may simply not have ticked, `?? documentToHtmlString(rawArticle.fields.content)` for a description Contentful never received. The body is rendered a second time only for the Articles that need it, because `??` never evaluates its right side when an authored description exists. Every default written here is asserted against the DTO layer, so removing one from the code fails the docs test.

## entities/ (Astro content collections)

```
entities/<plural>/
  <plural>.ts   # defineCollection({ loader, schema })
  index.ts
```

The loader is the only place content I/O happens. Most of them are the same steps with a few holes punched in them, so they share `cmsCollection` in `entities/collection.ts` and pass what varies: the query, the mapper, the ordering rule, the image field, and how an entry is identified. `tags` and `authors` keep hand-written loaders because they fetch more than one content type. The steps, whether a loader composes them or writes them out:

1. `fetchEntries<[Skeleton, …]>(query, …)`: one query per array of raw entries it answers with, in the order they were written, each typed by its Skeleton (`EntriesQueries<Skeleton, undefined>`), so `content_type` is that Skeleton's `contentTypeId`, a `select` entry names a field it has, and a literal that disagrees fails `tsc`. That call is the whole of what a loader knows about the CMS: no Effect, no `CmsClient`, no runtime, and no credential guard; no page cursor either, since `fetchEntries` pages until every matching entry is in hand. Missing credentials answer an empty array per query rather than failing, so builds work without them, and several queries run concurrently because the interface batches them, not because the loader asked
2. map with the concept's DTO, apply domain ordering rules (`sortFavoriteFirst`)
3. return entries carrying an `id`, whatever is unique for that concept, *not* automatically the slug: `articles` → `slug`, `tags` → `slug`, `authors` → `slug`, `cities` → `name`, `testimonials` → `quotee`, `projects` → `id` (the one `createProjects` derives from `fields.id`, trimmed, falling back to a slugified name when it is empty). Each pair is asserted against its loader. `cities` keeps the name because a City’s slug is `slugify(name)`, so the two are one identity by construction, and `testimonials` keeps the Quotee’s name, read from the CMS field Contentful still calls `author`, so two quotes from the same person collapse ([ADR 0019](../../docs/adr/0019-three-questions-before-modelling.md)). `cmsCollection` writes the spread before the key, `{ ...entry, id: identify(entry) }`, so a DTO carrying an `id` of its own (`ProjectDTO` does) cannot override the one `identify` chooses
4. `schema` always comes from `@domain/<concept>` and is bound as it is exported, never built, extended or redeclared here. `authors` binds `authorEntrySchema`, which is `authorSchema` plus the `latestArticle` reference an authors entry carries beyond the Byline, and `AuthorDTO` is inferred from it, so the field and its type are declared once

The tags loader emits one flat entry per addressable Slug, ordered Article references and all; the A–Z bucketing the tag listing renders is `buildTagIndexBuckets` (`@domain/tag/rules`), called from [`pages/tags/index.astro`](../pages/tags/index.astro).

Post-processing that *fetches* (`getImagePlaceholders`) happens in the loader, after the DTO, not inside it. Rewriting an image URL does not fetch, which is why the rich-text renderer in `dto/article/utils/content.ts` may do it inline. `withImagePlaceholders` in `entities/placeholders.ts` hands `getImagePlaceholders` every source at once and reads its answer, a `Map` keyed by the source it was given: a source missing from the `Map` reads as "no placeholder", so `placeholders.get(url)` is the whole of what a loader does with it.

**Testing a loader.** A loader is reachable from Vitest. [`entities.test.ts`](./entities/entities.test.ts) holds every loader to step 1 (they all answer with no entries and ask Contentful for nothing without credentials, and they all query once they have them), and `articles` and `authors` additionally have their own files covering mapping, ordering and post-processing. It costs two `vi.mock` calls per file: one for `astro:content`, and one that spreads the real `@infrastructure/cms/client` but swaps `CmsClientLive` for the stub layer in [`src/tests/doubles/cmsLayer.ts`](../tests/doubles/cmsLayer.ts). What `fetchEntries` promises on its own (one array per query, in order, concurrently, empty without credentials, and complete however many pages that takes) is asserted once, in [`cms/entries.test.ts`](../infrastructure/cms/entries.test.ts), where `cmsServesPagesOf` makes the double answer a `total` larger than the items it serves. What the test cannot check is the `schema`: `reference()` has no stand-in, so no entry is ever parsed. ADR 0016 records both halves.

## Adding a content type

domain concept (`schema`/`types`/`rules`) → `dto/<concept>` → `entities/<plural>` → register the collection in [`src/content.config.ts`](../content.config.ts). Add the glossary term to `GLOSSARY.md` in the same change.
